Mayflower

Mayflower is an unofficial academic planner for Colby College students on Mac, iPhone and iPad. It reads your own Colby records (Workday courses, degree progress, Moodle) after you sign in to Colby yourself, and helps you plan schedules and track requirements. It never registers for classes on your behalf.

Mayflower is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Colby College.
