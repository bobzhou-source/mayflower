Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026.

What we collect

To create a Mayflower account we store your email address and an account identifier with our authentication provider, Supabase. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address (or Apple private relay address) and identifier that provider shares. We use this only to sign you in and keep your workspaces separate.

What stays on your device

Your Colby academic data (courses, schedules, grades, degree progress, Moodle content), your saved Colby login, and the Colby session are stored only on your device, in the app's sandbox and the system Keychain. They are not sent to us or any third party. Mayflower talks directly to Colby's own websites (Okta, Workday, Moodle) from your device.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no sale or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your Mayflower account at any time from the Account screen in the app; this permanently removes your account from our authentication provider. Removing the app deletes the on-device data.
